# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target User

**The "Shadow IT" Operator:** We are not targeting "startups" generically. The ideal user is a **VP of Engineering, Head of Operations, or fractional CFO** at a Series A/B startup (20-150 employees). At this stage, founders have delegated credit card access, individual engineers are expensing $20/mo tools directly, and the finance team has lost visibility into redundant stack overlaps (e.g., frontend team buys Cursor, backend team buys Copilot, marketing uses ChatGPT, support uses Claude).

## The Trigger Moment

Right before they use this tool, they are:
1. Reviewing the monthly Ramp/Brex burn report and seeing $4,000 in "Software Subscriptions" with no utilization metrics.
2. Attempting to enforce a company-wide standard (e.g., "Everyone use Copilot") but facing pushback from a team using a different tool.

## Where They Hang Out

- **Slack Communities:** Rands Leadership Slack (specifically `#eng-managers` and `#finance`), Out-of-Pocket, YC Founder network (if applicable).
- **Subreddits:** r/SaaS, r/fatFIRE (many fractional CFOs lurk here), r/startups.
- **Newsletters/X:** They read Gergely Orosz (Pragmatic Engineer), Lenny Rachitsky, and follow folks like @GergelyOrosz and @shl.

## The First 100 Users (30 Days, $0 Budget)

**The "Benchmark Takedown" Strategy:**

1. **The Unfair Distribution Channel:** As Credex, we have access to aggregated (anonymized) data on what *everyone else* is paying. We publish a highly-contrarian report: *"Series A Startups are burning $18k/yr on redundant AI tools."*
2. **The Wedge:** We drop this report into the `#finance` and `#eng-managers` channels in Rands Leadership Slack, not as a pitch, but as a resource: *"We analyzed 50 companies' AI spend. Here's the benchmark. We built a free calculator to check if you're above or below the P50."*
3. **Cold Outreach to Fractional CFOs:** Fractional CFOs manage the books for 5-10 startups simultaneously. They are financially motivated to cut burn. I will scrape LinkedIn for "Fractional CFO SaaS", and send a direct, value-first cold email: *"Hey [Name], I built a tool that automatically finds redundant AI spend (Cursor vs Copilot overlap, retail API spend). Run it for your clients, look like a hero, save them cash. Takes 60 seconds."*
   - Acquiring 10 fractional CFOs could yield 50+ startup audits.

## Week-1 Traction Targets

If this works, by Day 7 we should see:
- **300 unique visitors** to the landing page.
- **60 completed audits** (20% conversion from visit to completion).
- **12 leads captured** (email submitted).
- **2 high-value consultations booked** (spend >$1k/mo).
