# Unit Economics

To justify maintaining StackAudit, we must ensure the customer acquisition cost (CAC) and conversion funnel lead to profitable Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) for Credex.

## Lead Value Estimation

Let's assume Credex's core business involves selling AI credits at a volume discount.
- **Average Customer:** A Series A startup spending $2,500/mo on AI APIs and tooling.
- **Credex Margin:** Assume Credex takes a 10% cut or markup on the volume discount they negotiate.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** $2,500/mo * 12 months * 10% = **$3,000/year gross profit** per converted customer.

## The Conversion Funnel

We need to model the drop-off from a cold visitor to a paying Credex customer.

1. **Top of Funnel (Visitors):** 10,000 visitors/month (via organic, viral sharing, and Fractional CFO outreach).
2. **Audit Completion Rate (20%):** 2,000 audits completed.
3. **Lead Capture Rate (15%):** 300 emails captured.
4. **High-Savings Qualification (30%):** 90 leads have >$500/mo in savings potential.
5. **Consultation Booking Rate (10%):** 9 consultations booked.
6. **Close Rate (33%):** 3 closed-won deals per month.

**Monthly Revenue Added:** 3 deals * $3,000/yr = **$9,000 in new ARR added per month.**

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

- **Development/Maintenance Cost:** ~$2,000/mo amortized (engineering time, Vercel/Supabase hosting).
- **Outreach/Distribution Cost:** $0 (Sweat equity via Slack communities and cold email).
- **Total Blended CAC:** $2,000 / 3 closed deals = **$666 per acquired customer.**

## Payback Period & Path to $1M ARR

With an LTV of $3,000/yr and a CAC of $666, the **payback period is under 3 months** ($666 / $250 MRR), which is exceptional for B2B SaaS.

**What has to be true to hit $1M ARR in 18 months?**
To add $1M ARR over 18 months, we need to acquire ~333 total customers, or **~18 customers per month**. 
Based on our funnel, scaling to 18 customers/mo requires scaling top-of-funnel traffic from 10k to **60k visitors/month**. This requires moving beyond zero-budget Slack outreach and heavily investing in the "Viral Audit Share" loop (where founders share their savings scorecards on X) and potentially running highly targeted LinkedIn ads against the "Fractional CFO" persona.
