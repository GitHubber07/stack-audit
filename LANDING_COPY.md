## FAQ

**Q: Why is this free?**
A: We're Credex. We help high-growth startups secure negotiated, volume-discounted AI infrastructure. We built this tool because most founders don't realize they are overspending in the first place. If you have significant savings potential, we hope you'll hire us to capture it. 

**Q: Do I need to connect my bank account?**
A: No. StackAudit requires zero integrations. You just tell us what tools you use, what plans you're on, and how many seats you have. We do the math using real-time vendor pricing data.

**Q: How accurate is the math?**
A: 100% accurate as of the current month. We hardcode and constantly update the pricing rules directly from the official pricing pages of OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub, Cursor, and Codeium.

**Q: Will you spam my email?**
A: Never. You don't even need to give us your email to see your results. We only ask for it *after* we show you value, if you want to save your report or book a consultation.

**Q: We only spend $100/mo on ChatGPT. Is this for us?**
A: Yes. StackAudit will likely tell you that your spend is highly optimized, give you a pat on the back, and let you get back to building. But it never hurts to check.
